Website privacy

Updated 7 September 2026

This page describes the Tableloom website at tableloom.cc. Website analytics does not access the desktop application or your .tableloom projects.

Your choice

Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Choosing “No thanks” keeps it off. You can change your choice at any time through .

Your choice is stored in your browser for 180 days. This preference works without sending it to Google.

Optional Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, Google receives information about page visits, visit duration, device and browser type, approximate location, referring websites, and clicks on the Windows download buttons. Analytics cookies distinguish visits; their lifetime is set to 180 days. Query strings and URL fragments are excluded from the page address we send.

Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. The website does not send names, email addresses, project files, or artwork to Analytics. Reports are managed through the site owner’s personal Tableloom Analytics account.

Google processes analytics information on its servers. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

Hosting and downloads

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Your browser contacts GitHub to load the site, check the latest Tableloom release, and download the application. These requests include normal connection information such as your IP address. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Fonts and screenshots are hosted with the website. Search Console verification adds a public ownership tag; it does not add a visitor tracking script.

Questions

Contact the maintainer through the Tableloom repository. Avoid posting personal information in public issues.
